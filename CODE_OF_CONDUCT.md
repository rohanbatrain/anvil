# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the person you are replying to is competent and acting in good
faith. Critique code, not people.

## Standards

Behaviour that helps: giving specific, actionable feedback; saying "I was wrong"
when you were; asking what someone was trying to achieve before telling them
they did it badly; and remembering that a contributor's first patch is usually
their most nervous one.

Behaviour that does not: harassment of any kind, personal or political attacks,
publishing others' private information, sexualised language or imagery, and
sustained disruption of discussion.

## Scope

Applies in the repository, its issues and pull requests, and anywhere someone is
representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to the maintainer listed in `pyproject.toml`. Reports are handled
privately. The maintainer may edit, remove or reject contributions that breach
this document, and may ban a contributor for sustained or serious breaches.
