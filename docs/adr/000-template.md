# ADR-0000: Title in the imperative

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by ADR-0NNN | Deprecated
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** who
- **Supersedes:** ADR-0NNN (if any)

## Context

What forced a decision. The constraint, the pressure, the thing that made
"carry on as before" untenable. Written so someone who joins in a year
understands the situation without having lived it.

## Decision

What was decided, in the active voice and one paragraph. "We will…".

## Consequences

What becomes true because of this — the good, the bad, and the merely
different. Be specific about the cost. An ADR with no downside listed is an
advertisement, not a record.

## Alternatives considered

Each one that was genuinely on the table, and the concrete reason it lost. This
section is the reason the document exists: the decision is usually guessable,
the rejected options never are.
